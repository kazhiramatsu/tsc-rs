## 5. Ladder placement and prohibitions

This rung is exactly: design gate, one runtime flip, first post-flip sweep,
and manifest freeze. It is the m-2 execution of the ladder described in
[`h2-6c.md`](h2-6c.md): m-1 supplied breadth machinery; m-2 closes the
divergence surface; the ca-1 analog freezes the closing evidence; the ca-2
analog wires acceptance and transition. The hosted boundary grows at ca-2,
after the green post-flip sweep, not in this packet.

The following are hard gates:

- No refusal lift without a frozen-evidence band covering its condition,
  option shape, suite, and fixture family.
- No lift of `declarationMap`, `emitDeclarationOnly`, `rootDir`,
  `declarationDir`, `outFile`, or `tsBuildInfoFile` in this rung.
- No manifest baseline from the pre-flip 501 proposal; the post-flip sweep
  is the only baseline.
- Manifest changes are shrink-only and facet-exact from observed output.
- No hand-authored expected bytes, diagnostics, emitted-file lists, maps,
  URLs, or result fields.
- Deferred-to-slices rows remain count-only and are not converted into
  manifest entries.
- A nonzero unclassified refusal, nondeterminism, activity leak, or changed
  denominator blocks the flip result and receives a named evidence update.
